Taking a Picture of the Quark-Gluon Plasma

Bose-Einstein correlations and Lévy sources in lead-lead collisions at 5.02 TeV, explained at an undergraduate level (7 min, English narration).

What the video covers

  1. A droplet of quark-gluon plasma
  2. Bose-Einstein correlations: a quantum ruler
  3. Not only how big, but what shape? Gaussian, Cauchy and Lévy sources
  4. One formula, three numbers: R, α, λ
  5. The CMS measurement
  6. Result 1: the source size R = 1.6 to 5.8 fm, and fluid-like scaling
  7. Result 2: the shape α = 1.6 to 2.0
  8. Result 3: the strength λ and the core-halo picture
Based on: CMS Collaboration, "Two-particle Bose-Einstein correlations and their Lévy parameters in PbPb collisions at √sNN = 5.02 TeV", Phys. Rev. C 109, 024914 (2024), doi:10.1103/PhysRevC.109.024914. Figures from the paper are reproduced under the CC BY 4.0 licence. This is an unofficial educational explainer, not a CMS Collaboration product.
